3-Line Calendar for Watch Face

Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026.

3-Line Calendar for Watch Face does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Calendar data

The app reads your calendar events on your device through Apple’s EventKit framework, to display your upcoming events on your Apple Watch face and in the app. This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to any third party, and it is not used for any purpose other than showing you your events.

No tracking, no analytics

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. It does not create an account, and it makes no network requests to transmit your information. Don’t take our word for it — the app is open source (Apache 2.0) and you can read every line at github.com/xinbenlv/three-line-calendar.

Data you choose to send us

If you email us for support, we receive the email address and any information you include, and use it only to respond to you.
