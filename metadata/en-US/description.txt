Your next three calendar events, always at a glance — on your Apple Watch face, your iPhone or iPad Home Screen and Lock Screen, and your Mac desktop.

3-Line Calendar does one thing well: it shows the next three timed events of your day as three clean lines — time and title. Nothing else.

WIDGETS EVERYWHERE
• Apple Watch: the original 3-line watch-face complication
• iPhone & iPad: Home Screen widgets (small, medium) and Lock Screen widgets
• Mac: desktop widgets (small, medium, large)

NO BS
• Free. No in-app purchases. No hidden charges.
• No account. No sign-in.
• No ads. No tracking. No network requests.
• Open source (Apache-2.0).

HOW IT WORKS
Events are read through Apple's EventKit, so every calendar on your device shows up — including Google Calendar and any other account you've added. All-day and multi-day events are skipped on purpose: you only see the timed events that shape your day. When today is done, you get a countdown to your next event.
